# Open Aqua Pain-Signal Register

**Status:** Discovery input, not aquarium-care evidence

**Captured:** 15 August 2026

**Source class:** Founder-supplied screenshots of a public freshwater hobbyist discussion and a founder-supplied review of publicly described aquarium-app capabilities

## Use boundary

This register preserves abstract owner problems only. The repository does not retain the screenshots, participant names, profile photographs, copied comments or competitor expression.

The supplied discussion had no reviewable source URL and contains conflicting anecdotal claims. It may shape research questions and safe workflow design; it may not establish a care threshold, diagnosis, dosing rule, product recommendation or scientific fact. Any future aquarium guidance still requires a controlled source record and qualified review.

## Resulting priority

1. Complete the working concern and measurement-quality loop, including real-device usability.
2. Finish Quiet Plan with filter, equipment and maintenance reminders that the owner explicitly approves.
3. Build a reviewed, deterministic stocking and compatibility pre-check before disease-related features.
4. Add outcome checks so persistent problems can be compared with what the owner actually tried.
5. Consider owner-controlled sharing and photo-assisted drafts only after the trusted private loop is proven.

## Explicit non-responses

Open Aqua will not respond to these signals by:

- diagnosing disease or plant deficiency from a photograph, symptom or single number;
- recommending a product, medication or dose from anecdotal discussion;
- telling an owner to add livestock merely to change a nutrient reading;
- publishing unreviewed universal “ideal” values;
- building a public attention feed; or
- copying competitor language, layouts, rules, screenshots or datasets.

## Seed acceptance scenarios

- A lone nitrate result at or above the screening trigger with no method context requests an exact repeat and source-water comparison.
- A protocol-checked repeated high result after a recorded water change asks about inputs before suggesting another large intervention.
- Low nitrate with healthy-looking plants does not produce dosing or stocking advice.
- Low nitrate with a plant concern requests light, carbon, fertilizing and symptom context without declaring a deficiency.
- Gasping creates a rapid welfare triage and qualified-help boundary without naming a disease.
- Cloudy water or increased algae asks for the smallest relevant history and water checks without guessing a cure.
- Cycling uncertainty requests a dated trend and never treats one result as proof of readiness.
